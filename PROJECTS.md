## Submitting your project

1. Finish your capstone in your own fork of this repo.
2. Make sure your fork's CI is green: `make test`, `make lint`, and
   `make fmt-check` all pass, and coverage is at least 70%.
3. Open a pull request from your fork back to this repo. Your PR should
   contain **one change only**: a new row in the table above, linking to
   your fork. Do not include your project's source code in this PR; that
   stays in your fork. A CI check on this repo will fail your PR
   automatically if it touches anything other than `PROJECTS.md`.
4. Fill out the pull request template checklist in full.
5. Once reviewed and merged, your project is listed here for the cohort
   and anyone visiting the org to see.
